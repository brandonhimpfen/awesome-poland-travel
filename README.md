## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, Schengen visa info, and meteorological tracking.

- [Poland Travel (Official Tourism Portal)](https://www.poland.travel/en) - Official national tourism board portal featuring regional destination highlights, cultural event calendars, and travel guides.
- [Ministry of Foreign Affairs - Visas](https://www.gov.pl/web/diplomacy/visas) - Official consular portal for Schengen visa entry regulations, passport requirements, and consular services.
- [IMGW Weather (Institute of Meteorology and Water Management)](https://imgw.pl/en) - Official national meteorological service providing weather forecasts, severe storm warnings, and climate radar data.

## Transportation

Navigating national railway networks, intercity trains, long-distance buses, and urban transit.

- [PKP Intercity](https://www.intercity.pl/en/) - National passenger railway operator managing high-speed Pendolino trains and intercity routes connecting major Polish cities.
- [Polregio](https://polregio.pl/en/) - Regional passenger rail network providing widespread local train connections across all voivodeships.
- [FlixBus Poland](https://www.flixbus.pl/) - Major long-distance coach network connecting regional towns and international European destinations.
- [Jakdojade](https://jakdojade.pl/) - Essential multimodal public transport planner and ticketing app for navigating trams, buses, and metro systems across Polish cities.

## Destinations & Regions

### Warsaw & Mazovia

- [Warsaw Tourism Office (GoWarszawa)](https://warsawtour.pl/en/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the reconstructed Old Town, Royal Route, museums, and modern skyline.
- [Warsaw Public Transport (ZTM Warszawa)](https://www.ztm.waw.pl/en/) - Official planner and ticketing guide for Warsaw's metro, tram, bus, and urban rail network.

### Kraków, Małopolska & Tatra Mountains

- [Kraków Official Travel Portal](https://www.krakow.travel/en) - Official visitor guide for exploring the Main Market Square, Wawel Royal Castle, and historic Kazimierz Jewish quarter.
- [Wieliczka Salt Mine Official Portal](https://www.wieliczka-salt-mine.com/) - Visitor ticketing and tour information for the UNESCO-listed underground salt cathedral near Kraków.
- [Tatra National Park (Tatrzański Park Narodowy)](https://tpn.pl/) - Official guide for alpine hiking trails, Zakopane mountain resorts, and protected Tatra peaks.

### Gdansk & Pomerania Coast

- [Gdansk Official Tourism Portal](https://visitgdansk.pl/en/) - Official travel hub for exploring the historic Royal Way, Motława riverfront, and Baltic coastal beaches.

### Wrocław & Lower Silesia

- [Wrocław Official Tourism Portal](https://visitwroclaw.eu/en) - Visitor guide for exploring Cathedral Island (Ostrów Tumski), market square gnomes, and Gothic architecture.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers and remote professionals taking advantage of Poland's tech ecosystem and visa programs.

- [Poland Business Harbour / National Visa Portal (Gov.pl)](https://www.gov.pl/) - Official government portal for IT professionals, startup founders, and skilled remote workers seeking residency.
- [HubHub Warsaw](https://hubhub.com/) - Popular premium coworking and innovation community space located in central business districts.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contacts, medical assistance, and local safety rules.

- [112 European Emergency Number](https://www.gov.pl/) - Integrated single European emergency hotline for police (Policja), fire brigade (Straż Pożarna), and medical rescue (Pogotowie Ratunkowe).
- [Mountain Volunteer Search and Rescue (TOPR / GOPR)](https://topr.pl/) - Specialized rescue service operating emergency response patrols in the Tatra and southern mountain ranges.
